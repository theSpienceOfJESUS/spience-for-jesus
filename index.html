What Is the SPIENCE Method?

What a Global Apostle Teaches About AI

Elder Gerrit W. Gong, an Apostle in a global Christian faith, released a one-hour teaching in June 2026 — "Faith, Dignity and Human Flourishing: Hearing God's Voice in an Age of Artificial Intelligence." Here are the 20 most important things he teaches:

1

AI answers questions. Only God answers prayers. A search engine finds facts. Only your Father in Heaven finds you.

2

AI organizes information — but it cannot give you revelation, covenant connection, or divine truth. Those come only from God.

3

God never hallucinates. He never makes things up. He always tells the truth. No AI can say the same.

4

Don't let AI shape your picture of God. Learn who He truly is through His Word and through the Spirit in your heart.

5

You are not a data point. You are a beloved child of God. No machine can measure your divine potential.

6

"Let AI inform, but you decide with the Lord." Your identity and your agency are sacred — they belong to you and God, not to an algorithm.

7

AI carries human bias. Faith leaders are needed to help anchor it with a moral compass.

8

Three guideposts for disciples using AI: Rely on the Spirit first. Practice wisdom second. Choose trusted sources third.

9

AI affects all four of your most important relationships — with God, yourself, others, and nature. Use it to deepen all four. Don't let it replace any one.

10

No one should sit alone with a chatbot. If you confide more in AI than in a friend — reach out to a real person today.

11

"Discipleship is a covenant journey. AI is a tool." A hammer helps build a house. But the hammer is never the home.

12

Use AI for logistics and drafts — not as a replacement for real relationships. Let it help you plan. Then spend your best energy with people.

13

Don't let AI do the work meant to help you grow. What you don't earn yourself, you don't keep in your soul.

14

Write these three truths where you see them daily: I am a child of God. I am a child of the covenant. I am a disciple of Jesus Christ.

15

As AI gets smarter at thinking, we must get better at being human — faith, compassion, love, humility, empathy, forgiveness.

16

Get off your phone and go outside. God's classroom is in creation. Nature teaches patience and joy in ways no screen ever can.

17

"May we never lose the divine intelligence that matters most: the voice of God." In a world of noise, learn to hear His whisper first.

18

Anyone, anywhere has the possibility to become anything. AI should expand this gift — not limit it.

19

Use the gift of the Holy Ghost to discern what is real vs. unreal. Pray before you share. Pray before you trust a source.

20

"Please choose wisely to follow Jesus Christ." No generation in history ever needed Him less. Ours is no different.

Which AI Tool Is Right for You?

There are many AI tools available today. Rather than telling you which one to use, we believe the most important first step is to ask God.

A Simple Invitation

Before you download any AI tool, find a quiet moment. Ask your Father in Heaven: "Which of these tools will help me come closer to Thee and serve Thy children better?" Then listen. The Holy Ghost will guide you.
